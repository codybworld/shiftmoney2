How the estimates work

The app estimates take-home pay by applying your chosen withholding percentage to tips and any other take-home pay you enter. When “Tips only” is selected, scheduled shift targets are targets for tips; when “Tips + estimated after-tax hourly wages” is selected, expected hourly wages reduce the tip target.

This is a planning estimate, not payroll, tax, legal, or financial advice. Actual taxes, tip reporting, paycheck timing, and deductions can differ. The app does not connect to a bank or calculate official tax liability.
